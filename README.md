# SkyRun-Cohort-Analysis
Customer analytics project using Python to analyse sales performance, customer retention, cohort behaviour, RFM analysis and customer segmentation, with actionable business recommendations.

## Project Overview

This project analyses customer purchasing behaviour and sales
performance using Python. It explores revenue trends, customer
retention, purchasing patterns and customer segments, then translates
verified findings into practical business recommendations.

## Business Objectives

-   Understand overall sales and revenue performance.
-   Identify important products and countries by revenue.
-   Examine monthly customer activity and purchasing trends.
-   Measure repeat purchasing behaviour through cohort analysis.
-   Evaluate customers using Recency, Frequency and Monetary (RFM)
    analysis.
-   Segment customers and identify opportunities to improve retention
    and revenue.

## Project Workflow

1.  **Data loading and inspection** -- load the CSV dataset and review
    its structure, columns and data types.
2.  **Data cleaning** -- inspect missing values and duplicates, prepare
    date and customer fields, and calculate sales value where
    appropriate.
3.  **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** -- analyse revenue over time,
    product performance, country performance and customer activity.
4.  **Cohort analysis** -- group customers by their first purchase
    month, calculate cohort index and measure monthly retention.
5.  **RFM analysis** -- calculate Recency (days since the latest
    purchase), Frequency (distinct orders) and Monetary (total customer
    spend).
6.  **Customer segmentation** -- group customers using the segmentation
    method implemented in the notebook.
7.  **Business recommendations** -- interpret the results and suggest
    actions based on observed patterns.

## Cohort Analysis

Cohort analysis groups customers by the month of their first purchase
and tracks whether they make purchases in later months. The cohort index
represents the number of months since the customer's first purchase.
Retention is calculated by dividing the number of active customers in
each cohort period by the number of customers in that cohort's first
month.

A blank cell in the cohort table or heatmap indicates that the relevant
later period is not available in the dataset; it should not
automatically be interpreted as zero retention.
